Support

Grimshell · iPhone, iPad and Mac

The quickest way to get help is to email magnusjune.dev@gmail.com. Say which device you're on and what you expected to happen, and you'll get a reply from a person.

What the app is for

Grimshell teaches the command line one command at a time — pwd, ls, cd, cat, chmod, grep, and the rest — in short lessons you can finish in a couple of minutes on a train. Everything is tappable; you never have to type unless you want to. Chapters end in a test that unlocks the next one.

Does it run real commands?

No. The terminal in a lesson is a simulation running against an invented filesystem. It cannot see or touch anything on your actual device, which is exactly what makes it safe to practise rm in.

Progress and syncing

Your progress saves automatically after every lesson. If you are signed in to iCloud, the same progress follows you between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. If it isn't appearing on a second device:

  • Check both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, in Settings at the top of the list.
  • Check iCloud Drive is switched on, under Settings › your name › iCloud.
  • Open the app on the first device and finish a lesson — progress is sent when it is saved, not on a timer.
  • Give it a minute on a decent connection.

If iCloud is off entirely, nothing breaks. The app keeps everything locally on that device.

Reminders

Grimshell sends at most one notification a day, at the hour you chose when you first opened it, and stops sending them after three go unanswered. If you declined notifications at first launch and want them back, turn them on in Settings › Grimshell › Notifications.

Sound

The mute switch is in the header on iPhone and iPad, and along the top of the window on Mac. Sound is set per device, so muting it on your phone leaves your Mac alone. Audio respects the ring/silent switch and will not interrupt music you're already playing.

Accessibility

Text scales with Dynamic Type throughout, and exercise layouts reflow rather than truncate at large sizes. The CRT flicker is switched off automatically under Reduce Motion.

A lesson looks wrong, or an answer isn't accepted

Email the address above with the chapter and lesson name and what you typed. Content mistakes are fixed in the next update.
