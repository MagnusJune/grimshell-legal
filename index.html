Privacy Policy

Grimshell · Last updated 22 September 2026

Grimshell does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. The app has no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

What the app stores

Grimshell keeps a record of how far through the course you are: which lessons and chapters you have completed, your experience points, your daily streak, the daily goal and reminder hour you chose at first launch, which commands are due for review, and whether sound is switched on. That is the whole of it. It is stored in a small file on your device, and — if you are signed in to iCloud — mirrored to your own iCloud account so the same progress appears on your other devices.

Who can see it

You can. Nobody else, including the developer, has access to it. The iCloud copy lives in your personal iCloud key-value store, governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot read it and never receives a copy. If you have iCloud switched off, the app works exactly the same and simply keeps everything on the one device.

The terminal is simulated

Grimshell teaches shell commands by simulating a shell. Commands you type in a lesson run against a small make-believe filesystem held in memory for the duration of that lesson. The app never executes a real command, never spawns a process, and never reads, writes, or lists any file on your device. The dungeon you are exploring is invented; your own files are not visible to it and cannot be reached from it.

What is not collected

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No crash-reporting or telemetry services
  • No third-party SDKs — the app has no dependencies at all
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or calendar access
  • No email address, name, or any other identifier collected by the developer

There is no sign-in and no account system. The app makes no network requests, so there is nowhere for your information to be sent.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications — a single daily reminder, at an hour you pick yourself, to come back and practise. It stops on its own after three are ignored.

That is the only permission requested. Declining it leaves the app fully usable; you simply will not be reminded.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove the copy synced to iCloud, use Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on your device. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held elsewhere to request the deletion of.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
